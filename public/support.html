About Castalia

Castalia (formerly InkWell) is a journal for people who think in circles, forget what they figured out last month, and want a place that holds all of it without judgment. For the Thinkers and the Forgetters.

What Castalia does

  • Private journaling that syncs across web, iOS, and Android
  • Voice journaling that turns speech into clean, readable entries
  • Sophy, an AI companion who offers prompts and reflections only when you ask
  • Five rotating, research-cited gratitude practices, plus Reframe and timed Sprints
  • WISH, a values-based goal method with a realistic runway
  • Weekly and monthly insights that connect dots across time (Plus)

Getting started

  • Create a free account, or sign in with Google or Apple
  • Start your first entry, typed or spoken, with a prompt if you want one
  • The free version is a complete journal; Plus adds the deeper intelligence layer if you want it

Privacy & data

Your words are yours. The short version:

  • Your entries are encrypted in transit and at rest
  • We never sell your data, and never use your entries to train AI
  • No one else can read what you write; Sophy reads your journal only when you ask her to, never in the background
  • You can export or delete your data at any time

For complete details, see our Privacy Policy.
