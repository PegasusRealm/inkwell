What's Inside

Life is messy. InkWell holds your thinking and gives you back insight.

Every capture mode is free. The intelligence layer is there when you want more.

Write however it comes out

Type it, or just talk. InkOutLoud turns your voice into a clean, readable entry automatically, so a thought caught on a walk doesn't die on a blank page later.

Ask your journal anything

Search by meaning, not just keywords. Ask "when did I feel most like myself this year" and InkWell finds the entries that answer it. Plus

Gratitude that doesn't go flat

Five distinct, research-cited gratitude practices on rotation, so the practice keeps working instead of becoming background noise.

Quick capture and longer Sprints

InkBlot is for the fast thought you don't want to lose. Sprints are timed, uninterrupted writing sessions for thinking something all the way through.

See the other side

Reframe walks you through a short, structured practice for looking at a hard moment from a different angle, in your own words.

Goals built from what you value

WISH is a values-based goal method. Sort what matters to you, name a goal that grows out of it, and get a plan with a realistic runway instead of a vague resolution.

See your year, not just your day

Browse entries on a calendar, and get weekly and monthly insight summaries that connect dots across time Plus

A simple, optional check-in

FeelCheck is a 1-to-5 rating you can attach before and after a practice if you want to notice a shift. Optional, never required, never a score.

Share on your terms

Generate a Practice Summary you can choose to share with a therapist or anyone you trust. Built from usage patterns, never the content of what you wrote.

Meet Sophy

A companion who reads only when you ask.

Sophy is InkWell's AI companion. She offers a prompt when you want one and reflects back what she notices when you ask her to. She does not read your entries in the background. Silence is the default.

When you do ask, she remembers. Sophy can bring back what you wrote months ago, dated and in your own words, when it's genuinely relevant to what you're working through today.

  • Reflections grounded in your own past entries, only when you ask
  • Prompts when you want a starting point, silence when you don't
  • Your entries are never used to train AI

Example reflection

I keep circling the same decision and getting nowhere.
You wrote something close to this in March, and back then the circling stopped once you named what you were actually afraid of losing. What would that look like this time?
